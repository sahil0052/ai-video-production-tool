# 🎬 Shot Recipes Reference: 12 Modular Motion Templates

A catalog of 12 production-grade shot templates for Remotion, GSAP, and Google Flow AI hybrid video pipelines.

---

## 1. Hero Hook Split Card (0-Second Split 50/50)
- **Role**: Immediate retention hook at frame 0.
- **Visuals**: Top: 3D Paper Diorama AI clip. Bottom: Talking head with clean headroom.
- **Audio**: Instant dialogue + punchy sub-bass thud (`click-soft.mp3`).

## 2. Tactile Vox Paper Collage Diorama
- **Role**: Concept metaphor explainer.
- **Visuals**: Full-screen 3D Google Flow Veo 3.1 diorama with aged newspaper textures, cardboard boxes, and directional lighting.
- **Audio**: Paper slide / whoosh transition (`card-slide-1.mp3`).

## 3. Interlocking Gear Formula Engine
- **Role**: Explaining compound formulas (e.g. `Global Gold ($) × USD/INR`).
- **Visuals**: 3D mechanical cardboard gears rotating with gold foil and green labels printing an output receipt.
- **Audio**: Subtle mechanical ratchet / tick SFX.

## 4. Rocket Price Spike / Multiplier Chart
- **Role**: Market surge or exponential growth.
- **Visuals**: Paper graph line exploding upward through cardboard clouds with spark particles and red currency drop arrows.
- **Audio**: Rising shimmer whoosh into cash rustle (`handle-coins.mp3`).

## 5. Detective Investigation Corkboard
- **Role**: Deep-dive analysis / problem breakdown.
- **Visuals**: Corkboard with pinned cards connected by red string, magnifying glass sweeping over comparison cards.
- **Audio**: Tactile pin place (`card-place-1.mp3`).

## 6. Financial Trading Terminal Live HUD
- **Role**: Live market rates / technical proof.
- **Visuals**: Cardboard trading monitors with glowing green checkmarks on `XAU/USD` and `USD/INR` exchange rates.
- **Audio**: Subtle keyboard clack + electronic chime.

## 7. SaaS Feature Bento Grid Showcase
- **Role**: Product feature breakdown.
- **Visuals**: 4-card bento grid with glassmorphic cards, active borders highlighting in rhythm with voiceover.
- **Audio**: Staggered pop SFX per card reveal.

## 8. Kinetic Typography Word Pop-In
- **Role**: High-energy subtitle emphasis.
- **Visuals**: Word-by-word spring pop-in (`mass: 0.5, damping: 10`), yellow highlight marker wipe across key keywords.
- **Audio**: Soft marker sweep SFX.

## 9. 3D Product Rotator with Glassmorphism Card
- **Role**: Hardware / physical product showcase.
- **Visuals**: 3D rotating isometric product render with floating glass spec pills.
- **Audio**: Ambient tech drone with low-pass filter.

## 10. Mathematical Formula & Geometry Morph (Manim)
- **Role**: Algorithmic proof or equation breakdown.
- **Visuals**: LaTeX equation morphing into geometric curves with coordinate grid alignment.
- **Audio**: Subtle mathematical sine chime.

## 11. Animated Comparison Metric Cards (Before vs After)
- **Role**: High-contrast performance or cost comparison.
- **Visuals**: Split cards sliding in from left and right with animated percentage tickers.
- **Audio**: Dual thud impacts (Red card thud -> Green card chime).

## 12. High-Impact Branded Outro Card
- **Role**: Final call-to-action and brand retention.
- **Visuals**: Dark glass card (`#10141CE0`) with gold/emerald shield monogram logo, sleek typography, and social handle pills.
- **Audio**: Final harmonic resolution bell (`success-chime.mp3`).
