# Viral Sound Design & Audio Grammar Skill

## 1. Core Principles
- **Retention Driver**: Sound is 50% of the visual experience. Audio prevents the "Dopamine Dip" every 1.5–2.5 seconds.
- **Mobile Speaker Tuning**: Boost presence between 2.5 kHz and 4.5 kHz; high-pass filter dialogue at 80 Hz; target -14 LUFS integrated.

## 2. The 4-Layer Hierarchy
1. **Dialogue & Voice (-6 to -10 dB)**: Primary message, high clarity, centered.
2. **Tactile Foley (-14 to -18 dB)**: Paper rustles, card slides, book flips.
3. **Kinetic Spot FX (-10 to -14 dB)**: Whooshes, pops, mechanical ticks, stamp thuds.
4. **Music & Ambience (-22 to -28 dB)**: Auto-ducked by 8–12 dB under dialogue.

## 3. Cue Mapping & Timing
- **0.00s Hook**: Sub-bass thump (50-80 Hz) + paper stamp thud.
- **Scene Cuts (1.5-2.5s)**: Air whoosh (0.15-0.22s) triggering 2-3 frames BEFORE the visual cut.
- **Numbers / Metrics**: Mechanical UI tick (2400-2800 Hz).
- **Questions / "Why?"**: Snappy bubble pop (1400-1600 Hz).
- **Mistakes / Risk Traps**: Exponential reverse riser (0.8s) + low alert drop.
- **Predefined Rules / Structure**: Clean switch toggle / stamp click.
- **CTA / Solution**: Harmonic bell chime (C6-E6).
