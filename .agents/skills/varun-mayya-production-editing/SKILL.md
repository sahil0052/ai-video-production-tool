## 2. The 6-Tier Semantic SFX Taxonomy

Every sound effect must be motivated by a specific visual or semantic trigger. Unmotivated SFX are prohibited.

| Tier | SFX Name | Acoustic Profile | Semantic & Visual Trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | **Hook Sub-Impact** | Low sub-bass thump (35–75 Hz) + crisp transient punch (0.6s) | Video start (0.0s), shocking revelations, dramatic title reveals. |
| **Tier 2** | **Transition Whoosh** | Fast airy sweeping noise with volume envelope (0.20s–0.28s) | Every camera push-in / punch zoom, every B-roll slide transition. |
| **Tier 3** | **Data / Code UI Tick** | High-frequency mechanical/digital click (2400–2800 Hz, 0.05s) | Statistics popping up, numbers counting, code lines writing, badges snapping. |
| **Tier 4** | **Question / Pop Snap** | Snappy high-mid digital pop / bubble snap (1400–1600 Hz, 0.12s) | Curiosity hooks, questions (*"Lekin Kyun?"*, *"Problem market nahi..."*). |
| **Tier 5** | **Risk Riser & Warning Drop** | Exponential reverse tension riser (0.8s) + ominous low drop | Leading into mistakes, danger warnings, leverage traps, compounding losses. |
| **Tier 6** | **Brand / CTA Chime** | Harmonic major triad bell chime (C6-E6-G6, 0.5s) | Payoff, brand logos, website mentions, *"Follow for free guide"*. |

## 3. Strict Scene-by-Scene Benchmark Criteria (Stage 4 QC Gate)

Before the video editing engine executes Stage 5 rendering, it MUST audit the planned scene inventory against these non-negotiable benchmarks:

1. **Visual Attention Reset Benchmark**:
   * A static talking-head frame must **NEVER exceed 2.2 seconds** without a visual reset (punch zoom, B-roll cutaway, motion graphic card, or floating telemetry badge).
2. **100% Entity Coverage Benchmark**:
   * 100% of named entities, numerical figures (e.g. *90%*, *1:500*, *1 Lakh*), financial terms (*Leverage*, *Drawdown*), and tools mentioned in the dialogue MUST have a corresponding visual artifact.
3. **Picture-in-Picture (PIP) Rule**:
   * Whenever full-frame B-roll or motion graphics occupy the screen for > 2.0s, the presenter MUST remain visible in a styled top-right circular PIP window (340×340 with glowing border) to maintain personal connection.
4. **Kinetic Captioning Standard (Varun Mayya Signature)**:
   * **Font:** Monospace Tech (`Share Tech Mono` / Consolas).
   * **Layout:** High-contrast `#0B1012` dark pill bounding boxes (92% opacity).
   * **Highlighting:** Word-timed Cyan (`#00E5FF`) and Gold (`#FFE600`) highlights on key emphasis tokens.
   * **Script:** Transliterated Romanized Hinglish for instant mobile readability.
5. **Audio Mastering Standard**:
   * Music bed ducked **`-6.5 dB`** during speech.
   * Master integrated loudness strictly normalized to **`-14.0 LUFS` ± 0.5 LUFS** and true peak capped at **`-1.0 dBTP`**.

## 4. Triple-Engine Asset Acquisition Protocol

* **Engine A: Real-World Evidence (Web Search & Fetch)**:
  * Used for official regulatory documents (CFTC, SEC), actual software interfaces (MetaTrader, GitHub, Bloomberg), and corporate logos.
* **Engine B: Cinematic Concept Imagery (`generate_image`)**:
  * Used for visual metaphors (e.g. futuristic AI trading robots in server vaults, financial storm charts).
  * Always generated in `9:16` vertical portrait with dark high-contrast styling.
* **Engine C: Procedural Motion Graphics Engine**:
  * Programmatic 1080×1920 video plates for live telemetry, candlestick charts, leverage scales, capital drain bars, and drawdown curves.

## 5. Vox & Google Flow Production Directives

When producing split-screen (`1080x960` top + `1080x960` bottom) Vox-style video deliverables, the following rules are strictly enforced:

1. **Presenter Framing (Snug Headroom & Zero Forehead Clipping)**:
   * Crop positioned at `y=320` placing hair right below the split divider with minimal empty wall space (~20–30px breathing room), preserving the full face, eyes, and shoulders with zero forehead clipping.
2. **Top Half Visuals (Edge-to-Edge Cover)**:
   * Top visual pane rendered in full screen edge-to-edge cover (`1080x960`) with zero pillarboxing, black bars, or blur padding.
3. **Zero Visual Repetitions (1-to-1 Unique Scene Mapping)**:
   * Every narrative beat (5–8s) MUST have its own unique 3D Google Flow scene. For a 50–60s video, generate 7–8 unique scenes so that no clip is ever looped or repeated.
4. **Selective Emphasis Punchline Captions Only**:
   * No continuous minor dialogue captions. Subtitles appear ONLY on 5–6 high-impact punchlines in extra-large Impact font (sizes 76–80) with bright yellow/red accents.
   * Transcribe/translate with clean English Latin alphabet (`task="translate"` in Whisper) with zero Arabic/Urdu characters.
5. **Exact Full Container Duration**:
   * Always match exact `ffprobe` source container duration (e.g., `57.61s`), with zero milliseconds truncated at the tail end.
6. **Pre-Delivery Automated QC Frame Audit**:
   * Sample keyframes every 2.0s across the complete timeline to verify dimensions (`1080x1920`), contrast, subtitle purity, and `-14.0 LUFS` loudness prior to delivery.
7. **Mandatory Google Flow Clip Generation & Halt Directive**:
   * Always generate genuine AI video clips (via Google Flow Veo 3.1 / Image-to-Video) for explainer and visual scenes.
   * **STRICT HALT RULE:** If you encounter ANY problem, blockage, or error while generating clips, you **MUST NOT** proceed with editing using fallback static images or alternative edits. You **MUST STOP immediately**, pause further editing, and report the exact error to the user first.
