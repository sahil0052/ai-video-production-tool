# Master Sound Design Grammar for Viral Short-Form Videos (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

> **Core Philosophy**: Sound is **50% of the visual experience**. In short-form video, audio is the primary retention driver that prevents the **"Dopamine Dip"** (the neurological point where a viewer gets bored and swipes away).

---

## 1. The 4-Layer Sound Design Hierarchy

Every viral timeline must be structured in vertical tiers with precise volume mixing:

1. **TIER 1: DIALOGUE & SPEECH (The Backbone) [-6 to -10 dB]**
   - High-pass filtered at 80Hz (removes mud)
   - De-essed & presence boosted at 3.5kHz (cuts through phone speakers)
2. **TIER 2: TACTILE FOLEY & PROPS (The Physical Anchor) [-14 to -18 dB]**
   - Paper rustles, card slides, book flips, tactile textures
   - Gives 2D digital elements physical weight and realism
3. **TIER 3: KINETIC SPOT FX & PATTERN INTERRUPTS [-10 to -14 dB]**
   - Fast whooshes, pop snaps, mechanical UI ticks, stamp thuds
   - Resets viewer brain attention every 1.5 – 2.5 seconds
4. **TIER 4: ATMOSPHERE, DRONES & MUSIC (The Mood) [-22 to -28 dB]**
   - Auto-ducked by 8–12 dB whenever speech is active
   - Swells during pauses, builds tension with risers

## 3. The 5 Golden Rules of Viral Sound Editing

### Rule 1: The "J-Cut Audio Lead" (2-Frame Pre-Hit)
- **Never trigger a transition sound *after* the cut happens.** 
- Place the whoosh or riser **2 to 3 frames BEFORE** the visual transition lands. The human ear processes auditory cues faster than visual motion, making the cut feel instantaneous and organic.

### Rule 2: The 2.5-Second Auditory Pattern Interrupt
- In high-retention TikTok/Reels, viewers experience a dopamine drop if no new stimulus occurs within 2.5 seconds.
- Alternate sound types: `[Sub Thump] -> [Whoosh] -> [Pop] -> [UI Tick] -> [Riser] -> [Stamp]`. Never use the same sound effect twice in a row.

### Rule 3: The Power of the "Micro-Drop Silence"
- Right before delivering a major reveal, shocking punchline, or core solution, **cut all background music and sound effects to 0 dB for 0.3 to 0.5 seconds**.
- The sudden silence forces the listener's brain to lean in, amplifying the impact of the next word 300%.

### Rule 4: Multi-Element Sound Layering (The Triad Formula)
Pro editors never use a single flat sound effect. Every major action is built from a 3-element stack:
1. **The Body (Movement)**: Air whoosh / paper thwip.
2. **The Transient (Impact)**: Sharp mechanical click / snap.
3. **The Weight (Bass)**: Low sub-thud (50–80 Hz).

### Rule 5: Master for Mobile Phone Speakers
- 94% of short-form viewers watch on smartphones with tiny, high-passed speakers.
- **Cut frequencies below 35 Hz** (wastes headroom without being audible).
- **Boost presence between 2.5 kHz and 4.5 kHz** (gives whooshes, clicks, and dialogue maximum clarity).
- Target integrated **-14 LUFS** with a **-1.0 dB True Peak** ceiling to prevent platform compression clipping.
